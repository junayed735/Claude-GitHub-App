How a project goes

  1. We talk

    A call about what your site needs to do, who uses it, and what isn’t working now. You get a written plan and a fixed price within three days.

  2. I design

    Designs for phone and desktop, with your real content. You see them in a browser, not a PDF, and we revise until they’re right.

  3. I build

    Clean, fast code, tested on real devices. You get a preview link to check progress whenever you like.

  4. We launch

    I put it live, show your team how to edit it, and fix anything that comes up in the first month at no charge.

About

I’m Junayed, a designer and developer based in Bangladesh. For more than ten years I’ve made websites for shops, clinics, studios and schools.

I do both halves of the work, design and code, so nothing gets lost between a pretty mockup and the thing that actually ships. I care about the details most people skip: how a page feels on a cheap phone, whether a screen reader can follow it, how fast it loads on a bad connection.
